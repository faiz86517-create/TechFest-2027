About TechFest

TechFest 2027 is an exciting college festival celebrating technology, creativity, innovation and student talent.

Our Featured Events

  1. CodeSprint: A programming competition testing coding and problem-solving skills.
  2. RoboRally: A robotics challenge where participants control robots through obstacles.
  3. PixelCraft: A digital poster designing competition.
  4. Battle of Bands: A musical competition for talented students.
  5. IdeaForge: A platform to present creative and innovative ideas.
